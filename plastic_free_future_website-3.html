01 / Understand the issue

What is plastic bottle waste?

Plastic bottle waste is made when bottles are thrown away instead of being reused or correctly recycled. Plastic does not disappear quickly. If waste is not managed properly, it can build up on land and in waterways.

🧴

Single-use bottles

Some bottles are used for only a short time before being thrown away.

🗑️

Too much waste

When many bottles are discarded, they add to the amount of rubbish that communities must manage.

🌊

Pollution

Bottles that are littered can be carried by wind and rain into drains, rivers, and the ocean.

02 / Why it matters

How can plastic waste affect nature?

Land + water

Plastic litter can make places dirty, block drains, and travel from streets into rivers and seas.

Wildlife and habitats

Animals can become trapped in plastic waste or mistake small plastic pieces for food. This can harm wildlife and disturb natural habitats.

People and communities

Polluted public spaces are unpleasant and can create extra work and costs for communities that need to collect and manage rubbish.

03 / Take action

Small actions, big changes

You do not have to do everything at once. Start with one practical habit and keep doing it.

Reduce

Choose a reusable bottle when it is safe and practical. Avoid buying a new single-use bottle when you do not need one.

Reuse

Use a suitable reusable bottle and clean it regularly. Follow the care instructions for the bottle.

Recycle

Check your local recycling rules. Empty the bottle and sort it correctly where bottle recycling is available.

Try this one-week challenge

For one week, notice when you use a disposable plastic bottle. When possible, choose a reusable option and learn where recycling is available in your area.
